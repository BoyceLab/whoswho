Overview Back to top

Gene lists disagree with one another. Clinical panels range widely in size, research lists use different inclusion rules, and NDD resources and epilepsy resources overlap without agreeing on labels. This page joins them on a single identifier so that overlap and disagreement are visible per gene, and attaches the organizations that serve each gene's community.

Method Back to top

Identifier

Every symbol on every list is resolved to an HGNC ID: current symbol first, then previous symbol, then an alias if it maps to exactly one gene. Symbols that do not resolve are written to a separate file rather than dropped. Copy number variant entries are kept apart from genes.

Tier

T1
ClinGen Definitive or Strong, any PanelApp Green, SysNDD Definitive, or Gene2Phenotype definitive or strong.
T2
Genes4Epilepsy inclusion, SFARI score 1 or 2, SysNDD Moderate, Gene2Phenotype moderate, PanelApp Amber, ClinGen Moderate, or a Simons Searchlight studied gene.
T3
Present on at least one list and none of the above.

A Refuted or Disputed label from ClinGen or SysNDD holds the gene at T3 and sets the disputed flag, whatever the other lists say.

Domain

epilepsy_primary: epilepsy sources describe epilepsy as the core phenotype.
ndd_with_epilepsy: the gene appears on both an epilepsy source and an NDD source, or an epilepsy source labels it DEE or developmental.
ndd_no_documented_epilepsy: NDD sources only.
systemic_with_seizures: malformation, metabolic, or systemic groupings with seizures as one feature.

Where the Wang et al. 2023 three-category classification is present it takes precedence.

Mechanism class

The mechanism label (channelopathy, synaptopathy, mTORopathy, chromatinopathy, cohesinopathy, and the rest) is an editorial grouping, not a source assertion. Where EpilepsyLive assigns a protein family by nomenclature, the class follows that family. Pathway groups that nomenclature cannot see (mTOR, synaptic vesicle, cohesin, neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis) come from an editor map, and the remaining genes are classed by symbol root. Each gene records which of the three applied, and the CSV can be edited to reclassify.

Organizations

The curated table is edited by hand and never overwritten by a build. Rosters and listed pages enter as candidates; a candidate is removed once a curated row exists for the same gene and the same website. The ORPHAcode on each gene row is the join key to disorder-level resources.

Code, configuration, and the curated table are in the repository behind this page. Suggest a gene or an organization by opening an issue there.
